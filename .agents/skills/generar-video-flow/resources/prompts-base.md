# Vocabulario y Bloques de Construcción de Prompts para Google Flow

## 1. Planos de Cámara (Camera Shots)
* `Over-the-shoulder shot` (Plano sobre el hombro mirando el celular/pantalla)
* `Close-up shot on hands holding a smartphone` (Primer plano de manos sosteniendo el celular)
* `Medium shot of cashier behind the POS counter` (Plano medio de la cajera tras el mostrador)
* `Point of view (POV) looking at a receipt printer` (Punto de vista mirando la impresora de tickets)
* `Low-angle tracking shot` (Plano contrapicado con movimiento suave)

## 2. Acciones Físicas
* `Scanning a barcode on a product box using a barcode scanner gun`
* `Handing a printed receipt to a smiling customer across the wooden counter`
* `Reviewing a physical paper supplier invoice while holding a modern smartphone`
* `Tapping on a smartphone camera aimed at a physical receipt on the counter`
* `Smiling proudly looking at the camera inside a well-organized neighborhood store`

## 3. Dispositivos con Pantalla Verde (Para Filmora)
* **Celular Vertical:** `holding a sleek modern smartphone with a solid chroma key green screen facing the camera, clean matte green display (#00FF00)`
* **Monitor Desktop:** `viewing a desktop computer monitor with a solid chroma key green display on a clean checkout desk`
* **Tablet:** `holding a 10-inch POS tablet with a solid bright green screen`

## 4. Ambientes y Fondos
* `Authentic Latin American neighborhood convenience store (tienda de barrio / minimarket), shelves stocked with colorful groceries, warm ambient lighting`
* `Cozy cafe and restaurant counter, coffee machine in background, soft bokeh, warm golden hour light`
* `Bustling retail shop checkout area, neat product displays, modern commercial lighting`
